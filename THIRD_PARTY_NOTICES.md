# Third-party notices

Each installer, ZIP and container image includes the applicable license texts
and generated `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. The notices cover locked Rust and npm
dependencies; container notices also cover the operating-system components.
Use the notices belonging to the exact version you downloaded.

The inventory is generated from locked dependencies, including the vendored
Tiberius driver. Container operating-system notices remain in their installed
copyright/license directories. The release inventory covers these components
as well as application dependencies.

Oracle Instant Client and IBM Db2 client/ODBC drivers are **not bundled**.
Obtain compatible drivers from their vendors and follow their license and
redistribution terms. unixODBC does not include these vendor drivers.

Database names and logos identify their respective vendors and do not imply
endorsement. Product screenshots use demonstration data.
